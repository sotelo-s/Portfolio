      ### MAReS: An Annotated Multimodal Corpus of ADHD Data from Reddit
      
      **Abstract**

      While corpora documenting linguistic markers of mental health conditions such as depression and anxiety are well established, corpora dedicated to Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) remain comparatively scarce and have received less attention. For that end, we introduce MAReS, the Multimodal ADHD Reddit Set, a dataset comprising nearly 2 million posts and comments from 6,847 Reddit users, along with 14,656 images. We adopt a conservative annotation strategy for positive cases, requiring explicit self-disclosure by users. To assess the agreement between this criterion and LLM-based annotation, we further conduct an LLM-as-a-judge analysis under two settings: one that accounts for sarcasm and one that does not.
      
      We provide an in-depth exploratory linguistic analysis and a set of baseline classification results across three paradigms: BoW, Transformer encoder-only models (including ADHDoBERTa, a domain-adapted variant), and zero-shot prompting with LLMs.
