# Profile

## About me

I hold a degree in Computer Engineering from the University of Santiago de Compostela and a Master's degree in Big Data. Throughout my academic and professional journey, I have developed a solid technical foundation in programming, data analysis, and artificial intelligence, alongside a strong interest in scientific research and its application to real-world problems.

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Information Technology, focusing on natural language processing. My research interests lie particularly in applying NLP techniques to social media data, bringing together my interests in large-scale data analysis, artificial intelligence, and the social impact of technology.
